Meet the owner

Lajpat Parihar

My dada, Jasnath ji, wove his first charpai in our village in 1985. I used to sit next to him and hand him the rope. He never measured anything with a tape; he counted with his fingers, and the bed came out perfect every time.

That is why the shop carries his name. I started this work in Jodhpur because I saw how many families in nearby villages could not find a good charpai at a fair price. We keep it at ₹699 so any family can afford one.

If you cannot come to us, we come to you. Sit with us while we weave, and you will see exactly what you pay for.

  • Every charpai is woven by hand, not by machine
  • Starting price of ₹699, told upfront

  • Rural craft knowledge passed down from my grandfather
